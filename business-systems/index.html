What we build

One system for the job that wastes the most time. These are the most common.

  • Car workshops

    Job cards with photos and claim numbers. The owner gets a WhatsApp when the car is ready.

  • Schools and tutors

    Who owes what in fees, polite reminders to parents who agreed to get them, and receipts.

  • Clinics

    An appointment book with reminders and a no-show list. Appointments only, never medical details.

  • Suppliers and hardware

    A quote desk that turns a builder’s list into a quote quickly, and follows up the ones that go quiet.

  • Colleges

    Every applicant from first enquiry to paid, so nobody is forgotten during intake.

  • Law and accounting offices

    Matters, deadlines and a list of the documents each client still owes you.

  • Transport

    Bookings, driver updates and a quick answer to “where is my load?”.

  • Spas and guesthouses

    A booking diary with deposits paid or owed, and the no-shows.

What every system includes

Simple to use on the first day, with no training course needed.

  • A login for each person

    Staff use their own phone or a computer. You decide who sees what.

  • Clear steps

    Every job, learner or booking shows where it is, at a glance.

  • WhatsApp updates

    Customers who agreed get a message at each step, so they stop calling to ask.

  • Photos and notes

    Keep the picture of the damage, the signed form or the delivery, with the record.

  • Search

    Find any customer, plate number or learner in seconds.

  • A monthly report

    One page for the owner: what came in, what is stuck, what is owed.

  • Export to Excel

    Your records are yours. Download them whenever you like.

  • Made for phones

    Big buttons, and fast on a slow connection.

How it works

You know your business. We do the building.

  1. A 30-minute visit

    Show us how you do the job today. We ask what goes wrong and what it costs you.

  2. A one-page plan and price

    What the system will do, who uses it and the full price, in writing. Free, with no obligation.

  3. We build it and show you

    You try a working version early, with your own steps and prices, and we change what you ask.

  4. We train your staff

    On site, in your office. Then we check in every month. Nothing goes live until you’re happy.

Yours, and private

  • In your name

    It runs in your own account. Once it’s paid for, the system is yours, and so is every record in it.

  • Only what’s needed

    We keep only the details the job needs. Clinic systems hold appointments only.

  • You decide who sees what

    Each person has their own login, and you can remove anyone at any time, including us.

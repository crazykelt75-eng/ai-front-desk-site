Questions owners ask

Will my customers know they’re talking to an assistant?

We set the greeting with you. We recommend saying it’s your assistant, so customers know a person will step in whenever they need one.

What happens when it doesn’t know the answer?

It says so, tells the customer someone will reply, and sends you a short summary on WhatsApp. Then it goes quiet in that chat, so you can take over.

Do I need a new phone or a new app?

No. You keep using WhatsApp as usual. Booking requests and questions for you arrive on your own WhatsApp.

Which number does it answer?

A WhatsApp number in your business’s name, on WhatsApp’s official business platform. Today that’s either a new number just for the assistant, or your current number moved off the WhatsApp Business app. We’ll help you choose. Either way, the number stays yours.

What does it cost?

From P500 a month. Founding clients pay no setup fee and get the first 14 days free. Meta also charges a small fee per reply after the first 1,000 replies each month, and bills you for that directly.

Does it understand Setswana?

Customers can write in English or Setswana. We test both with you before your assistant goes live.

How long does it take to set up?

First you send us your prices, hours and booking rules. Then we spend about two hours with you to connect your number, and we test the assistant before any customer sees it.

Can I change my prices later?

Yes. Send us the change on WhatsApp and we update your assistant. We also check it every week.

Is my customers’ information safe?

Customer messages are stored securely and used only to run your assistant. We never sell them or use them for advertising, and we follow Botswana’s Data Protection Act, 2024. Customers can reply STOP at any time.

Is it safe for clinics?

Yes. For clinics it only handles bookings, hours, directions and medical aids. It never gives medical advice and never sends results.

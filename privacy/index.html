Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

AI Front Desk is run by Kelvin Thale in Francistown, Botswana. We set up WhatsApp assistants that answer customer messages for local businesses, such as salons, spas, schools and clinics.

Contact: WhatsApp +267 76 200 519, email thalekelvin@gmail.com.

When you use this website

If you send the form on this website, we receive your name, your business name and type, the phone number or email you give us, how you’d like us to contact you, and your message.

  • We use these details only to reply to you and to set up the demo you asked for.
  • To stop spam, we keep a scrambled code made from your internet address for one day. We don’t keep the address itself.
  • This website has no advertising or tracking cookies, and it doesn’t load fonts or scripts from other companies.
  • If you tell us to stop contacting you, we stop and mark your details so we never contact you again.

When you message a business that uses AI Front Desk

We receive and store:

  • your WhatsApp number and profile name
  • the messages you send and the replies you receive
  • booking details you share, such as the service, day and time

We use them to answer your questions about the business’s prices, services and hours, to pass your booking request or question to the business owner, and to stop messaging you if you ask us to. The business you messaged sees your booking requests and any questions passed to them.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Meta (WhatsApp) delivers the messages.
  • Supabase stores messages and website requests on servers in the European Union.
  • Google (Gemini) helps write the assistant’s replies. Your message text is sent to Google for this, and Google’s own terms apply to that processing.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and website requests only as long as we need them to serve you or the business you contacted. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices and rights

Under Botswana’s Data Protection Act, 2024, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data.

  • To stop messages from an assistant: reply STOP. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • To delete your data: send “delete my data” to +267 76 200 519 on WhatsApp, or email thalekelvin@gmail.com. We will delete your messages and details within 30 days and confirm when it’s done.

Health and beauty businesses

The assistant only gives prices, hours, directions and booking help. It does not give medical advice and never sends results.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

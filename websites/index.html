What your website includes

We keep it simple: the things customers look for, done well.

  • Your services and prices

    Laid out clearly, so customers know what you charge before they ask.

  • Hours, a map and directions

    So people can find you, and know when you’re open.

  • WhatsApp and call buttons

    One tap opens a chat with you or rings your number.

  • An enquiry or booking form

    If you want one, so customers can send you a request any time.

  • Made for phones first

    Big text and buttons, and fast to load on a slow connection.

  • Easy for Google to read

    Clear page titles, your town and a map link, so search engines understand what you do.

  • Your own web address

    Such as yourbusiness.co.bw, registered in your business’s name.

  • No adverts

    Your website shows your business, not someone else’s.

How it works

You give us the facts. We do the building.

  1. Send us your details

    Your services, prices, hours, area and a few photos. A logo if you have one.

  2. We build a first version

    We show it to you on your phone, so you see what your customers will see.

  3. You check every word and price

    We change what you ask. Nothing goes live until you’re happy.

  4. It goes live on your own address

    We connect your web address and test it on phones before we hand it over.
